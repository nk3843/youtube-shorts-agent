Task:
- Given a "topic" string (e.g. "party games for Thanksgiving dinner"), write a YouTube Shorts voiceover script.

- Length: 30–45 seconds of spoken content.

- Style: direct, energetic, but not cringey. No long explanations.

  - 1 line hook

  - 1 line closing / CTA (e.g. "Save this for later" or "Try this at your next dinner")

- Do NOT ask the user questions.
- Do NOT include any scene descriptions or camera notes.
- Output ONLY the final script text, one line per beat.
